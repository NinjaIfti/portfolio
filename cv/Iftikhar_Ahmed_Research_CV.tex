\section{Publication}
\small
\textbf{Iftikhar Ahmed}, Mueid Islam Arian, Nusrat Jahan Lisa.
``Mel-Spectrogram Representations for CNN Based Adversarial Defense for Audio Deepfake Detection.''
In \textit{Proceedings of the 11th International Conference on Machine Learning Technologies (ICMLT 2026)}, Germany. IEEE, 2026.\\[2pt]
\href{https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11689539}{\faExternalLink*\ IEEE Xplore} \quad
\href{https://doi.org/10.1109/ICMLT69916.2026.11689539}{DOI: 10.1109/ICMLT69916.2026.11689539}
\begin{tight}
  \item Designed and evaluated a CNN-based (Xception) adversarial defense for audio deepfake detection. Mel-spectrograms
        reach an EER of 0.034 against 0.053 for LFCC, a 36\% relative improvement, across seven neural vocoders on WaveFake.
  \item Implemented adaptive FGSM/PGD adversarial training, reducing EER under FGSM attack from 0.70 to 0.107 and
        under PGD attack from 0.90 to 0.154.
\end{tight}
