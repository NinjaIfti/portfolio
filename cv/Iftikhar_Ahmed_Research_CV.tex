\section{Research Interests}
\small
Trustworthy machine learning: models that stay reliable under adversarial attack, distribution shift and
limited measurement. I work on three threads:
\textbf{robust and cross-lingual audio deepfake detection};
\textbf{AI for radiotherapy and oncology} (target delineation, medical image segmentation, multimodal outcome prediction);
and \textbf{verifiable quantum machine learning}.

{North South University \,\textperiodcentered\, Supervisor: Dr. Md Adnan Arefeen \,\textperiodcentered\, With Salman Shafi and Jarinah Tasnim}
